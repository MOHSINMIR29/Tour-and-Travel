About Kashmir

paradise on Earth

Cradled between the Pir Panjal and the Great Himalayas, Kashmir has long been called "Paradise on Earth" — and one visit is usually enough to understand why. Mist-covered valleys, alpine meadows, glacial rivers, and terraced saffron fields unfold one after another, each turn in the road revealing another postcard view.

Srinagar's Dal Lake is the region's beating heart, where centuries-old houseboats float beside Mughal gardens like Shalimar Bagh and Nishat Bagh, and Shikaras drift past floating vegetable markets at sunrise. A short drive away, Gulmarg turns into one of Asia's finest ski destinations in winter and a rolling green golf-course-in-the-clouds come summer, while Pahalgam's pine forests and the Lidder River make it a favourite base for gentle treks and pony rides.

Kashmir's culture is just as layered as its landscape — Kashmiri Wazwan feasts, hand-knotted pashmina shawls, papier-mâché art, and the warm hospitality of its people (mehmaan nawazi) make every trip as much about people as it is about scenery. The best time to visit runs from March to October for gardens and meadows in full bloom, and December to February for snow-covered peaks and skiing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what travellers usually ask us before booking.

What is the best time to visit Kashmir?

March to October is ideal for gardens, meadows, and lakes in full bloom, while December to February suits travellers looking for snow and skiing in Gulmarg.

Do I need a special permit for Ladakh?

Indian nationals need an Inner Line Permit for certain areas like Pangong, Nubra, and Tso Moriri; foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit. We handle the paperwork as part of your Ladakh package.

What is usually included in the package price?

Accommodation, private transport for the itinerary, and sightseeing as listed on each package are included. Flights, meals outside the plan, and personal expenses are usually extra unless mentioned.

Can I customize an itinerary or combine destinations?

Yes. Every package can be adjusted for duration, hotel category, or combined with another region — send us your requirements via the enquiry form or WhatsApp and we'll tailor a plan.

How do I book and what are the payment terms?

Submit an enquiry through the site or WhatsApp, confirm your itinerary with our team, and secure your booking with an advance payment; the balance is typically due before or during the trip.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

Cancellation and rescheduling terms depend on how close to the travel date you cancel and vary by hotel/vehicle partner; our team will share the exact policy for your package before you pay.
